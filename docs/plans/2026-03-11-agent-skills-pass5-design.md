# Agent-Skills Pass 5 Design

## Goal

Absorb the highest-signal documentation, research, feedback, and standards-review rules from `sources/agent-skills` into broad skills without importing source agent brands, AUTORUN markers, journals, or ecosystem-specific protocols.

## Source Focus

- `scribe`
- `researcher`
- `prose`
- `voice`
- `canon`
- `quill`

## Target Mapping

### Product And UX

- absorb research-question-first discipline
- absorb evidence strength, segment scope, and limitation reporting
- absorb feedback synthesis and user-facing content direction
- absorb voice/tone, error-copy, accessibility-text, and translation-readiness considerations where they affect UX direction

### Planning And Scoping

- absorb audience-aware document framing
- absorb requirement traceability and testable acceptance language
- absorb decision-to-influence framing from research planning
- absorb handoff-ready document expectations instead of loose planning prose

### Quality And Review

- absorb documentation-as-delivery review expectations
- absorb standards-citation and clause-based assessment discipline
- absorb evidence-backed compliance gap review without turning the skill into a legal or audit framework
- absorb documentation coverage and stale-reference review where the seam changed

## Explicit Exclusions

- agent names, handoff headers, and branded protocols such as `SCRIBE_TO_*`
- AUTORUN and `_STEP_COMPLETE`
- `.agents/*` journaling and calibration loops
- Japanese-only output requirements
- product-specific standards catalogs that would overfit the broad skill

## Convergence Rules

- keep each slice inside one broad skill
- prefer compact additions to existing assets and references over new sprawl
- merge only reusable operating rules, not source taxonomy
- require final verification from the main tree after convergence
